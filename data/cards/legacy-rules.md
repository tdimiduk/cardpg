# Legacy Rules

To interpret some cards and mechanics you may see from the csvs (or propageted into our yamls if we haven't updated them yet), here is some context on how the rules looked when a lot of our card data was written.

## v0.0.5 rules

Some of our playtest characters (like the swashbuckler and the shield-fighter) and a lot of the monsters were written for the v0.0.5 ruleset and have not been updated to the current ruleset yet in many cases.

In cards from this era you will encounter a shorthand for the colors: |x| -> red, |y| -> yellow, |b| -> blue.

### Defending from hand

In an earlier playtest version (where pc's like swashbuckler and shield-fighter were written) defense was done significantly differently:

Characters defended with cards from hand instead of their deck. There was no concept of "impact" instead you compared strength of the defense to strength of the attack:

---

| Defense >= Attack | No Effect |
| Defense >= Attack/2 | Minor Consequence |
| Defense >= Attack/4 | Major Consequence |
| Defense < Attack/4 | Critical Consequence |

---

Minor/Major/Critical consequences were kind of like our severity 1/2/3 consequences.

In this system, you had an "implicit defend" action, where you just played cards from your hand and totaled strength in the appropriate color to compare to the attack strength.

You could also use a Defend card from your hand which would offer you a different formula, maybe adding a numeric bonus, or allowing you to use a different color, or something else.

### Armor

In this version of the rules, armor allowed you to flip cards from your deck and use them towards a defense before you paid cards from your hand. Light armor allowed 1 flip and heavy armor allowed 2 flips
