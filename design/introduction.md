# caRdPG: Grounded Heroism

caRdPG is a role-playing game about grounded heroes, earned victories, and the stories we forge when we push past our limits. In this game, your accomplishments are meaningful because they are born from your decisions, your sacrifices, and your ability to adapt when the situation changes.

---

### The Art of the Trade-off

This is not a game of simple pass-fail checks, nor is it a meatgrinder designed to punish you. Instead, caRdPG is driven by **deliberate decisions, prioritization, and pacing**. Every challenge you face is a puzzle of trade-offs, forcing you to weigh what matters most:

- **Moment-to-Moment:** _Do I play my cards to strike now and hope it is decisive, or do I hold them back to ensure I can parry whatever my opponent throws at me next or deliver a knockout blow in a few rounds?_
- **Approach vs. Preparation:** _Do we execute a direct, high-stakes plan and deal with the immediate heat, or do we spend time planning, investigating, and breaking a massive threat down into smaller, low-risk tasks?_
- **Pushing vs. Rest:** _Do we press forward through our mounting weariness to beat a ticking clock, or do we pause to catch our breath, fully aware that the world’s threats will keep moving while we rest?_

---

### The Deck is Your Character

In caRdPG, your character is not a list of static numbers on a spreadsheet. **Your character is a 24-card deck.** This deck is a unified, elegant system that models three distinct aspects of your hero at once:

1. **Your Training and Capabilities:** The cards in your deck _are_ your skills, actions, and maneuvers. The numbers printed on them represent your core physical and mental attributes.
2. **Your Tactical Presence of Mind:** The hand of cards you hold represents your immediate focus. It reflects the fleeting opportunities, sudden openings, and split-second ideas you can capitalize on in the heat of the moment.
3. **Your Endurance and Attrition:** The depth of your deck is your overall reserve. As you exert yourself, your deck runs thin. Weariness is represented by **Fatigue** cards that slip into your deck, temporarily cluttering your hand and representing slipped concentration, heavy breathing, and fading focus.

---

### Narrative Arcs of Recovery

When a challenge completely overwhelms your defense, the result is an **Injury**. In caRdPG, injuries are not abstract points subtracted from a pool, nor are they permanent, crippling scars that ruin your character.

Instead, injuries are **narrative hurdles**. An injury is a physical card placed on the table in front of you—like a _Shattered Collarbone_ or a _Consuming Fever_—that temporarily changes how you play. It presents a new tactical problem for your entire party, forcing them to protect you, adapt their plans, and eventually seek out the time and resources to help you mend. Once treated, you fully recover, and your hero is left stronger and wiser for having survived the trial.

---

### The Tempo of Play

To support this style of storytelling, the game flows between two natural tempos:

- **Adventuring Time:** Used for travel, investigation, and downtime. Play is open-ended, and you maintain a small "Ready" hand face down on the table to react to sudden events.
- **Crisis Time:** Used when combat begins or a high-stakes hazard emerges. The turn-order stack is discarded; instead, everyone plans secretly and acts simultaneously in a fast-paced, breathless sprint.
