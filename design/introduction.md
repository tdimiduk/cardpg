caRdPG is a role-playing game about how legends are forged: through perseverance, daring, and the will to push forward when the deck is stacked against you. You and your friends will tell stories of capable but grounded heroes whose triumphs are earned, not given.

# Earn Your Victories

In this game, the story never stalls. The central question is not if you will succeed, but what you are willing to risk to achieve your goals. The tension comes from the difficult choices you make—how much effort to expend and what complications to risk. Your accomplishments are meaningful because of the trials you endure. The rules provide possibilities, not prohibitions; any course of action is possible if you are willing to accept the consequences. Powerful deeds are balanced by their costs and the consequences they risk, not by arbitrary restrictions.

# Trust the Story

The system is designed to be intuitive. An action that would make sense in real life (with the addition of magic) will be an effective choice in the game. You are encouraged to think like your character and engage with the world's fiction. If you can describe a dramatic or clever action, the rules exist to help determine what happens next, not to stand in your way.

# Your Deck is Your Resolve

Your character's inner strength is represented by a 24-card deck. This is your reserve of stamina, your focused training, and your heroic drive, all in one. There are no hit points in this game. Instead, your defense is an active struggle where you expend cards from your deck to parry a blow, leap out of the way, or brace your shield against an impact. Defending yourself is never without cost; while you may avoid a wound, the effort always drains your stamina and you may suffer tactical setbacks scrambling to avoid a mighty blow. This exertion of combat and adventuring will add Fatigue cards to your deck, creating a realistic rhythm of action and recovery that grounds your hero in the world. But when your defense is truly overwhelmed, the result is a significant injury: a card on the table in front of you. This is a persistent challenge that imposes real limitations until you are able to treat it and heal.

# Defining Moments

The rules allow your group to control the narrative's pace. Most of the time, you'll be in Adventuring Time, resolving challenges quickly. But when the stakes are high, you'll enter Crisis Time. Here, every action is declared and resolved simultaneously, creating a tense, tactical spotlight for the moments that will define your legend.

Welcome to caRdPG. Go forth and forge your story.
