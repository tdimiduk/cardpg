# Defense Action

This document is a rough draft, attempting to capture some of our thinking of how we want "Defend" actions on cards to work in the current defense mechanics. For context, see [legacy-rules.md](../../data/cards/legacy-rules.md) for the old system.

## Playtest observations

When playtesting the current defense mechanics, we found that the flip from deck mechanic played pretty well (and avoided the feel bad situation in the previous version of the rules where a player would have to spend the cards they were saving up to play to save themself from an attack).

However, we lost a nice feature from the previous system that players felt like they had a lot of agency in their defense and tactics of when and what cards to keep in their hands.

We have a few ideas for how to give players more agency in their defense.

### Play ahead of time

Some somewhat imprecise things we used in the last playtest

- Action: Put in play. Attack 10 {red} anyone who attacks you. At the end of resolution
  expend this if it triggered, recover to hand if not
- Action: put in play. The next 2 attacks against you, ignore the resource number on the top card, then expend this.

### Reactive defend from hand

The idea here is you will get an incomming attack with color and strength. Then, before you start flipping for defense, you may play `Defend` cards from hand. They will not count towards the impact of the defense, but they will add their strength in the relevant color to the defense. They can also have other rules (like letting you use a different color value if it is higher, or adding a modifier, using two colors, etc.)

We have also played with cards that you put into play ahead of time
