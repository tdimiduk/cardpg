# Benchmark Scenario: The Blighted Apothecary & The Quarantine Riot

## Scenario Overview

This scenario explores a biological and eldritch medical emergency within the walled Old Canal district of the metropolis of Oakhaven. An outbreak of **Ash-Spore Blight**—a fast-acting, petrifying fungal pathogen originating from deep subterranean catacombs—is spreading through the tenement populace. Infected victims develop brittle, crystalline spore growths beneath their skin, suffer violent coughing fits that spray infectious ash, and lapse into petrified comas within twenty-four hours.

The municipal council has responded with brutal pragmatism: chaining the canal gates, raising timber barricades around the district, and threatening to incinerate the entire quarter with alchemical fire if the infection is not contained before dusk.

The protagonists are an alchemical physician (a master of pharmacology, pathology, and laboratory extraction) and a runic abjurer (a disciplined scholar of eldritch wards and alien psychic phenomena). The day’s desperate lifecycle unfolds across four distinct beats: conducting forensic bio-investigation within the pestilential quarantine ward, high-risk alchemical synthesis under a ticking clock where mental strain risks neurological burnout, a chaotic non-lethal riot at the quarantine barricades where heroes must disperse a panicked mob without taking innocent lives, and a nocturnal vigil of deep psychological convalescence.

This scenario serves as the primary benchmark for **mental and cognitive condition bleed**—testing how neurological shock, psychic migraines, and tremors selectively degrade precision and intellectual tasks while leaving raw brute-force actions functional.

## Beat 1: Morning Investigation in the Quarantine Lazaretto

### Narrative Context & Stakes

The sun hangs pale and sickly over the misty canals. The district’s converted chapel serves as an overflowing lazaretto. Dozens of coughing citizens lie on straw pallets, their skin graying with fungal crystallization. The city bailiffs outside have already set up fire-thrower cauldrons along the barricade walls; the heroes have until noon to identify the precise spore strain, isolate its catalytic reagent, and locate the source of the outbreak.

The physician and abjurer don oiled leather coats, bronze beak-masks stuffed with crushed mint and camphor, and thick horsehide gloves. Inside the dark, spore-choked crypt beneath the chapel, they examine the body of the apothecary who was patient zero, search his preserved specimen jars and coded journals, analyze the fungal growth vectors across his organs, and attempt to comprehend the strange alien geometry of the subterranean spores.

### Actions & Colors Tagged

- `Painstaking Search` ([[Blue]] - Exploration): Methodically examining the apothecary’s cluttered laboratory, looking past mundane clutter to find hidden false-backed shelves and preserved spore cultures.
- `Interpret and Decipher` ([[Blue]] - Exploration): Translating the apothecary’s frantic, spore-smeared shorthand notes and archaic astrological marginalia.
- `Comprehend the Alien` ([[Blue]] - Exploration): Focusing arcane perception to study the bizarre crystalline growth pattern of the spores, grasping a non-terrestrial biological logic without succumbing to horror.
- `Endure Hardship` ([[Red]] - Downtime / Exploration): Breathing through suffocating, hot filtration masks for hours while handling toxic, bio-hazardous fungal fluids.

### Pacing Mode

`General Action (Adventuring Time)`. Exhaustive, methodical investigative labor. The heroes possess the intellectual training and protective equipment to conduct the search safely; resolution determines the precision of their findings, the mental toll incurred, and whether their physical protections hold against contamination.

### Plausible Narrative Fallout Spectrum

- **Clean Outcome:** Definitive forensic breakthrough. The physician identifies the active fungal enzyme and its mineral solvent; the abjurer deciphers the apothecary’s formula for a counter-catalyst; protective gear holds flawlessly; both enter the laboratory with sharp minds and clear purpose.
- **Minor Friction:** Strained senses and physical discomfort. The suffocating masks and caustic alchemical fumes cause painful respiratory burning and throat irritation (`Singed Airways / Coughing Fits`); deciphering the alien script causes severe ocular fatigue; minor delay eating into the synthesis clock.
- **Severe Consequence:** Spore breach and psychic contamination. The apothecary's journal carries a dormant psychic spore that bursts upon opening; the abjurer suffers violent mental backlash while staring into the crystalline structure (`Cognitive Shock / Dissonance`); the physician’s glove tears on a broken vial, requiring painful caustic cauterization to prevent infection (`Cauterized Hand`).

### Engine Brainstorming Prompts: Coupling & Cross-Domain Bleed

1. **Intellectual Strain as Universal Fatigue:**
   - Hours of forensic dissection, microscopic inspection, and decoding cryptograms in a sweltering protective suit burns immense energy.
   - Does intense intellectual labor contribute to the same universal fatigue that swing-swords or mountain climbs produce? When the physician leaves the lazaretto, are their legs heavy and their reaction times slowed simply from mental exhaustion?
2. **Environmental Toxicity vs. Physical Armor:**
   - Plate armor and shields provide zero protection against microscopic spores or airborne psychic resonance.
   - How does the engine treat specialized protective gear (beak-masks, oiled robes, camphor seals) without inventing whole new subsystems for disease gear? Can tools and gear provide dedicated mitigation for environmental hazards just like armor does for swords?

## Beat 2: Midday Research & High-Risk Alchemical Synthesis

### Narrative Context & Stakes

Noon. The church bells toll across Oakhaven. Outside the sealed quarter, city siege engines are positioning barrels of wildfire. The heroes retreat to an abandoned glassblower’s workshop to brew a vaporous counter-agent—a distilled curative aerosol that can be dispersed through the quarter's fog to neutralize the spores in both the living and the air.

Synthesizing forty gallons of the compound in two hours requires volatile, high-pressure laboratory work. The formula demands rare reagents: quicksilver salts, powdered star-iron, and distilled essence of moon-lily. Worse, the mixture must be bound using an unstable psychic flux: the abjurer must mentally commune with the eldritch resonance of the spore entity to trick it into absorbing the mineral poison.

When the glass distillation coil begins to rattle violently under immense supernatural vapor pressure, the physician must forcibly wrestle the bronze pressure valves shut while the abjurer opens their mind to the howling psychic frequencies of the fungal hive-mind.

### Actions & Colors Tagged

- `Conduct Magical Research` ([[Blue]] - Downtime): Synthesizing disparate alchemical notes into an emergency distillation formula under intense clock pressure.
- `Use Alchemy` ([[Blue]] - Downtime): Operating precision distillation alembics, balancing delicate thermal gradients, and measuring toxic reagent ratios.
- `Sacrifice Sanity` ([[Blue]] - Downtime): Deliberately opening one’s mind to the alien, maddening consciousness of the subterranean fungal spore to decipher its bonding frequency.
- `Wrest Control` ([[Red]] - Combat / Downtime): Physically forcing shut a bucking, high-pressure brass valve on the boiling alembic to prevent an alchemical explosion.

### Pacing Mode

`General Action / Extended Task Under Clock Pressure (Adventuring Time)`. An extended laboratory synthesis where every minute counts, failure risks massive catastrophe, and success demands pushing mental and emotional limits to the edge.

### Plausible Narrative Fallout Spectrum

- **Clean Outcome:** Masterwork synthesis. The aerosol cures crystallize into stable vapor-salts; thirty dispersal canisters are filled cleanly; the abjurer seals the psychic connection without lasting trauma; the cure is ready with an hour to spare.
- **Minor Friction:** Volatile batch with minor injuries. A glass tube cracks, spraying caustic acid onto the workbench; the physician burns hands and forearms while saving the flask (`Singed Flesh / Acid Burns`); the abjurer suffers a persistent ringing in their ears and visual halos (`Strained Synapses`); twenty canisters salvaged.
- **Severe Consequence:** Catastrophic psychic and neurological burnout. The abjurer’s mental ward collapses during communion; alien fungal harmonics flood their consciousness, inflicting a devastating neurological condition (`Arcane Migraine / Trembling Hands / Visual Hallucinations`); the physician must smash the primary alembic to stop a lethal explosion, barely distilling fifteen unstable doses from the wreckage.

### Engine Brainstorming Prompts: Coupling & Cross-Domain Bleed

1. **The Crucial Neurological Asymmetric Bleed Principle:**
   - If the abjurer suffers `Arcane Migraine`, `Trembling Hands`, and `Visual Hallucinations` in Beat 2, **how does this condition selectively bleed into subsequent actions?**
   - **Selective Impairment:** An arcane migraine and shaking hands makes fine-motor and intellectual tasks agonizing:
     - Trying to make a precision ranged shot (`Attack with Precision`) is severely penalized.
     - Trying to decipher complex runes (`Interpret and Decipher`) or maintain an intricate social persona (`Master a Persona`) is nearly impossible.
   - **Selective Independence:** But what about raw musculoskeletal or emotional actions?
     - If the abjurer needs to brace a wooden beam to hold a barricade (`Hold a Position`) or drag a heavy cart of medicine (`Carry a Burden`), their muscles and skeleton are undamaged! Adrenaline and brute force can still push through a headache.
   - How should an engine model conditions that cripple fine-motor finesse and intellectual focus while leaving brute-force physical endurance intact?
2. **Voluntary Sacrifice for Clocks:**
   - Actions like `Sacrifice Sanity` represent a character trading mental well-being to beat an external deadline. How does the engine encourage players to make these dramatic, self-sacrificing choices?

## Beat 3: Afternoon Non-Lethal Crisis: The Barricade Riot

### Narrative Context & Stakes

Three o’clock in the afternoon. Rumors that the city council is bringing up wildfire cauldrons have driven the quarantined populace into a frenzy of terror. Hundreds of desperate citizens—armed with iron crowbars, butcher cleavers, and heavy cobblestones—swarm the canal bridge barricade where the medical relief station is located. They intend to batter down the iron gates and break out of the quarantine zone, unaware that city archers on the outer walls are notched with flaming arrows ordered to shoot anyone who sets foot on the bridge.

The heroes arrive with their carts of curative vapor canisters. If the mob breaches the gate, they will be massacred by city soldiers, and the infected among them will spread the ash-blight across the entire metropolis. The heroes cannot use lethal steel or destructive fireballs against terrified, innocent townsfolk.

They must deploy the vapor canisters, create disorienting non-lethal sonic barriers, push back the mob’s vanguard with shield and stave, tend to trampled citizens caught in the surge, and rally the crowd with booming moral authority before the city archers open fire.

### Actions & Colors Tagged

- `Appeal to Passion` ([[Red]] - Social): Standing upon a wagon bed with booming voice, appealing to the crowd’s love for their children and declaring that the cure is here in their hands.
- `Overwhelm the Senses` ([[Red]] - Social / Combat): Detonating a thunderous acoustic flash-powder charge to shock the rioting mob into stunned, motionless silence.
- `Avoid Harm` ([[Yellow]] - Combat): Weaving through thrown cobblestones, lunging pitchforks, and surging bodies without raising a weapon in retaliation.
- `Administer Aid` ([[Yellow]] - Combat / Exploration): Swiftly pulling trampled victims from beneath the mob’s boots and administering the vapor aerosol directly into their lungs.
- `Command and Coordinate` ([[Blue]] - Combat / Social): Directing parish volunteers and lantern-bearers to form protective cordons around the distribution wagons and channel the crowd into orderly queues.

### Pacing Mode

`Crisis (Crisis Time)`. Fast, high-stakes tactical confrontation operating round-by-round. The challenge is uniquely non-lethal: the heroes must defeat panic, protect fragile medical gear, and absorb physical blows without killing their opponents.

### Plausible Narrative Fallout Spectrum

- **Clean Outcome:** Total pacification and mass cure. The acoustic charge halts the surge; the physician’s speech breaks through the panic; orderly distribution lines form; hundreds inhale the curative vapor; the city soldiers lower their bows; the riot ends without bloodshed.
- **Minor Friction:** Bruised and battered success. The crowd surges forward, smashing several vapor canisters and bruising the heroes with clubs and stones (`Bruised Ribs / Sprained Wrist`); the heroes pacify the crowd only after exhausting their voices and physical endurance; half the quarter receives the cure, halting the panic.
- **Severe Consequence:** Tragic escalation and heavy trauma. The mob crushes the outer barricade; city archers fire a volley, killing three citizens; the heroes throw themselves into the line of fire to shield the crowd, taking non-lethal bludgeoning and arrow wounds (`Concussive Blunt Trauma / Arrow Grazes`); the mob scatters in horror, with many fleeing into alleys uncured.

### Engine Brainstorming Prompts: Coupling & Cross-Domain Bleed

1. **Carrying Cognitive Impairment into Tactical Chaos:**
   - If the abjurer entered this riot with `Arcane Migraine` and `Trembling Hands` from Beat 2, how does that manifest during the chaos of the riot?
   - The abjurer cannot aim a precise spell without agonizing skull pain; their coordination (`Command and Coordinate`) is muddled by visual halos. Does the engine force them to rely on raw presence (`[[Red]]`) or physical resilience to contribute?
2. **Non-Lethal Constraints in Crisis Time:**
   - Most combat engines assume characters want to kill their enemies as efficiently as possible. In this scene, killing a rioter is an immediate moral and narrative failure.
   - How does the engine reward or support non-lethal tactical options (`Overwhelm the Senses`, `Administer Aid`) during Crisis Time without making players feel artificially handcuffed?

## Beat 4: Night Sanctuary & Cognitive Convalescence

### Narrative Context & Stakes

Midnight. The quarantine gates remain shut, but the district has fallen quiet. Curative vapors hang in the misty air, neutralizing the spores. In the chapel undercroft, the sick are breathing easily, their crystalline lesions softening and sloughing away as gray ash. The threat of wildfire has passed.

For the heroes, the physical danger is over, but the mental cost remains. The abjurer sits in a secluded vestry, head cradled in trembling hands, suffering the horrific aftershocks of having opened their consciousness to the alien fungal hive-mind. Bizarre geometric patterns pulse behind their eyelids, and whispering alien thoughts echo in their ears.

The physician brews willow-bark and valerian decoctions, lights soothing beeswax candles, and leads the abjurer through grounding meditative disciplines to purge the lingering cognitive intrusion and rebuild their shattered focus.

### Actions & Colors Tagged

- `Resist Mental Intrusion` ([[Blue]] - Downtime): Actively fighting off the lingering telepathic echoes of the alien hive-mind through iron mental discipline and meditative anchoring.
- `Heal a Spiritual Wound` ([[Blue]] - Downtime): Applying psychological grounding, empathetic conversation, and psychiatric herbalism to soothe a mind fractured by eldritch contact.
- `Indulge Vice (Intellect)` ([[Blue]] - Downtime): Restoring equilibrium by engaging in rigorous, comforting logic: cataloging mundane chemical equations, reading classic poetry, or playing methodical chess games.

### Pacing Mode

`Downtime`. Deep, quiet psychological recuperation. Time spent tending to internal trauma rather than external threats.

### Plausible Narrative Fallout Spectrum

- **Clean Outcome:** Total mental restoration. The psychic echoes dissolve; the alien geometry fades; meditative focus cleanses the abjurer’s mind; a night of profound sleep flushes all neurological strain, leaving behind valuable scientific insight.
- **Minor Friction:** Lingering psychic hangover. The migraine subsides into a dull, persistent ache; disturbing dreams disrupt sleep; the abjurer remains sensitive to loud noises and bright light into the following day.
- **Severe Consequence:** Persistent psychic scarring. The alien consciousness leaves an indelible imprint (`Eldritch Phobia / Fragmented Memory`); the abjurer cannot sleep without nightmares; mental focus remains brittle, requiring weeks of quiet academic therapy to fully heal.

### Engine Brainstorming Prompts: Coupling & Cross-Domain Bleed

1. **Downtime Healing for the Mind vs. the Body:**
   - While broken bones heal with splints and time, how does an engine structure the healing of psychic and cognitive trauma?
   - What makes `Heal a Spiritual Wound` or `Indulge Vice (Intellect)` mechanistically distinct from physical rest, and how do they interact with a character's long-term capabilities?
