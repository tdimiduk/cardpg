# Benchmark Scenario: The Sunken Aqueduct & The Vault of Echoes

## Scenario Overview

This scenario traces a perilous wilderness expedition into the snowbound spine of the Wyrmtooth Crags. The protagonists—a high-country ranger scout, an arcane scholar versed in ancient architecture and runic magic, and an armored mountain vanguard—have set out to locate and breach the Vault of Echoes. Within this cyclopean pre-cataclysm ruin, an apocalyptic cabal known as the Chime of Midnight is conducting a profane resonance ritual to awaken a slumbering elemental colossus entombed in the mountain’s heart.

The arc spans a grueling alpine ascent, meticulous pre-crisis reconnaissance of ancient wards, a desperate combat and ritual interruption crisis, and a grueling extraction where physical trauma turns routine mountaineering into a struggle for survival. It tests how an engine manages **Exploration**, **Combat**, and **Downtime** pillars, specifically focusing on the severe physical consequence bleed from environmental hazards into combat, the trade-offs of pre-crisis casing, and the burden-drag of hauling wounded companions out of peril.

## Beat 1: Morning Ascent & The Broken Aqueduct Crossing

### Narrative Context & Stakes

Dawn in the high crags brings sleet, biting wind, and freezing mist. To reach the secluded cliff-face housing the Vault of Echoes, the party must traverse the ruins of an ancient aqueduct: a colossal span of weathered masonry arching 200 feet above an ice-choked gorge. A central 40-foot section has collapsed, leaving only a frost-coated, sagging timber balk and crumbling keystone ledges.

The scholar deciphers the geological stress lines and shifting wind shears off the peaks, directing the scout where to drive iron pitons into stable stone. The scout moves with extreme care across the slippery timber span, trailing a woven safety line. When a jammed block of frozen masonry threatens to collapse the landing ledge on the far side, the vanguard uses crowbar and raw muscle to lever the hazard into the chasm before the rest cross.

### Actions & Colors Tagged

- `Endure the Elements` ([[Red]] - Exploration): Withstanding the biting sub-zero alpine winds and sleet without succumbing to debilitating numbness.
- `Chart Treacherous Paths` ([[Blue]] - Exploration): Reading the ice-coated rock formations, wind vectors, and structural decay of the ancient aqueduct to plot a viable route.
- `Tread Lightly` ([[Yellow]] - Exploration): Crossing the narrow, frost-slick timber beam and crumbling ledge without slipping or triggering structural collapse.
- `Break/Dismantle` ([[Red]] - Exploration): Forcibly wedging and prying free a dangerous overhanging keystone block to clear a secure anchor point.

### Pacing Mode & Friction Band

- **Pacing Mode:** `General Action (Adventuring Time)`. The party consists of seasoned mountain travelers with proper gear (pitons, ropes, woolens, crampons). Success in crossing is assumed; the resolution determines how much time and stamina the crossing demands, and whether anyone suffers physical harm or gear loss along the way.
- **Friction Band:** `Taxing / Moderate Friction`
- **Cost & Stamina Expectation:** Noticeable physical stamina drain under sub-zero wind and sleet. Specialists cross successfully but feel the weariness later; likely incurs 1–2 Severity 1 conditions (chilled limbs, shivering, minor rope strain).
- **Abort & Failure Dynamic:** Pushing recklessly across frost-slick keystones risks Severity 2 physical trauma (cracked ribs, sprained shoulder). Players can abort a hazardous crossing attempt to set redundant pitons or wait out a wind gust at the cost of valuable time against the ritual clock.

### Plausible Narrative Fallout Spectrum

- **Clean Outcome:** Flawless alpine teamwork. Anchor lines are driven true; the team crosses safely with minimal delay; stamina expenditure is well within routine march reserves.
- **Minor Friction:** Costly physical exertion. Sleet drenches gloves and boots; the scout slips midway and dangles on the safety line, bruising shins and taking chilling cold (`Chilled Limbs / Shivering`); an iron piton shears off; crossing takes an extra hour, advancing the ritual clock in Beat 3.
- **Severe Consequence:** Traumatic environmental hazard. The keystone shifts during the crossing; the vanguard leaps to catch the scout, catching the ledge hard and suffering a wrenching shoulder sprain or cracked ribs (`Bruised Ribs / Twisted Shoulder`); a pack containing spare climbing line is lost down the gorge; the party arrives at the vault entrance chilled, aching, and physically compromised.

### Engine Brainstorming Prompts: Coupling & Cross-Domain Bleed

1. **The Physical Bleed Principle (Environmental Harm Directly Degrades Combat):**
   - In stark contrast to social embarrassment (which has no physical reality on bone and muscle), **environmental harm suffered on the mountain directly and violently impacts combat performance in Beat 3**.
   - If the scout suffered a wrenched ankle on the frost-beam or the vanguard cracked a rib catching the rope, how does that physical trauma impair subsequent combat actions like `Avoid Harm` (dodging), `Land a Mighty Blow` (generating kinetic force), or `Hold a Position` (bracing against an impact)?
   - Why must an engine allow biological trauma suffered during travel to penalize physical agility and defense in combat, while preventing non-physical complications from doing the same?
2. **Time vs. Safety Trade-Offs:**
   - Taking time to chip ice away, place double anchors, and build a windbreak ensures physical safety but consumes precious hours. If the cult ritual in Beat 3 is advancing on a real-time clock, how does the engine make players feel the tension between **spending time to preserve health** versus **rushing carelessly to arrive before the ritual completes**?

## Beat 2: Midday Pre-Crisis Casing of the Outer Sanctum

### Narrative Context & Stakes

The party reaches the cyclopean threshold of the Vault of Echoes: towering basalt gates carved with resonant flute-grooves that hum in the mountain wind. The heavy stone doors stand slightly ajar. Inside the vaulted vestibule, cultist sentinels patrol the gallery catwalks while hound-sized cavern beasts gnaw on bones near the lower braziers. Deep within, rhythmic bronze gong strikes reverberate through the floor.

Rather than charging through the gates blind, the party spends half an hour establishing a covert vantage point. The scout crawls among the shattered friezes to map sentinel patrol intervals. The scholar examines the ancient wall reliefs to determine the sanctum’s architectural layout, finding forgotten acoustic flues that channel sound, and uses an arcane sensory focus to project consciousness into the high vaulted ceiling. They discover that the ritualists are drawing power from three resonant crystal stalactites suspended over the central dais.

### Actions & Colors Tagged

- `Observe and Plan` ([[Blue]] - Exploration): Methodically mapping sentinel movement patterns, blind spots, and defensible choke points in the outer antechamber.
- `Move Unseen` ([[Yellow]] - Exploration): Stalking silently along shadow-draped columns and rubble piles without alerting the sensitive guard beasts.
- `Remote Sensing` ([[Blue]] - Exploration): Projecting magical awareness up into the vaulted shadows to scout the interior layout and detect arcane conduits.
- `Read the Scars` ([[Blue]] - Exploration): Analyzing chisel marks, ancient impact fractures, and resonant acoustic grooves to deduce structural weak points and the ruin’s original defensive mechanisms.

### Pacing Mode & Friction Band

- **Pacing Mode:** `General Action (Adventuring Time) / Pre-Crisis Setup`. Deliberate, methodical reconnaissance aimed at converting time, observation, and stamina into decisive tactical leverage before entering life-or-death confrontation.
- **Friction Band:** `Routine / Low Friction` (with opt-in push into `Taxing`)
- **Cost & Stamina Expectation:** Minimal stamina burn for basic covert mapping from shadows; specialists locate blind spots cleanly. Opting to push into deep arcane resonance sensing burns focus and risks Severity 1 cognitive strain (`Strained Focus / Ringing Ears`).
- **Abort & Failure Dynamic:** Methodical observation allows clean disengagement at any moment; pushing too aggressively into active ward fields risks triggering acoustic alarms.

### Plausible Narrative Fallout Spectrum

- **Clean Outcome:** Pristine intelligence. The party maps the sentinels' blind spots, identifies the three resonant stalactite conduits, and locates an elevated archer’s perch overlooking the ritual dais, entering the crisis with complete surprise and tactical superiority.
- **Minor Friction:** Partial discovery with strain. Projecting consciousness through the resonant stone causes an echoing headache (`Strained Focus / Ringing Ears`); casing takes longer than anticipated; a patrolling sentinel catches a glimpse of movement, raising the inner guard to a state of heightened suspicion.
- **Severe Consequence:** Alarm triggered. The arcane scouting touches an ancient defensive ward, causing a resonant gong tone to chime through the chamber; the scholar suffers psychic backdraft (`Echoing Shock`); sentinels shout in warning and loose their beasts; the party is forced to charge into the sanctum immediately without tactical surprise and with enemies fully deployed.

### Engine Brainstorming Prompts: Coupling & Cross-Domain Bleed

1. **Reconnaissance as Crisis Leverage:**
   - How should an engine translate successful General Action casing into Crisis advantages?
   - Does knowing the layout grant the party immediate initiative, lower enemy target numbers, create dedicated "tactical asset" cards on the table (e.g., `Elevated Perch`, `Identified Conduit`), or allow them to nullify enemy surprise rounds?
2. **The Cost of Preparedness:**
   - Casing the sanctum burns mental discipline, sensory focus, and daylight. If a scholar exhausts their deep intellectual focus (`[[Blue]]`) during reconnaissance, does that leave them with less mental endurance to cast complex spells during the melee? How does the engine prevent pre-crisis scouting from feeling like a mandatory chore or, conversely, a cost-free buff?

## Beat 3: Afternoon Combat & Ritual Crisis: Stopping the Awakening

### Narrative Context & Stakes

The party bursts into the Grand Resonant Sanctum. At the center of an amphitheater of black basalt, an armored hierophant chants at an altar while cult acolytes channel humming sonic energy into a gargantuan, 30-foot humanoid effigy of carved granite and bronze veins: the Slumbering Colossus. The effigy’s eyes are already glowing with dull amber luminescence; hairline fractures spread across its stone chest as its massive arms begin to twitch. Cult champions with two-handed polearms advance to intercept the party.

The stakes are absolute: if the ritual concludes, the colossus will rise and pulverize the heroes and the mountain pass alike. The party must divide tasks under intense physical danger: the vanguard holds the narrow causeway against the cult champions, the scholar channels counter-magic and coordinates tactics, and the scout maneuvers through crossfire to physically shatter the harmonic conduits feeding the colossus before it fully awakens.

### Actions & Colors Tagged

- `Disrupt a Ritual` ([[Yellow]] - Exploration / Combat): Timing a precise shot or physical strike at the exact crescendo moment to shatter the harmonic crystal conduits.
- `Land a Mighty Blow` ([[Red]] - Combat): Delivering a crushing two-handed strike to shatter a cult champion’s heavy shield and break their line.
- `Channel Raw Magic` ([[Red]] - Combat): Unleashing a surge of unshaped arcane force to blast apart a wave of charging temple zealots at high personal stamina cost.
- `Use Tactical Magic` ([[Yellow]] - Combat): Snapping off a quick burst of kinetic grease or blinding flare to disrupt an archer’s aim on the high gallery.
- `Command and Coordinate` ([[Blue]] - Combat): Analyzing the chamber’s acoustic vectors and shouting rapid tactical directions to guide the vanguard’s defensive pivots.
- `Exploit a Weakness` ([[Blue]] - Combat): Identifying a fracture along the colossus’s left knee joint where ancient seismic damage can be leveraged to topple it.

### Pacing Mode & Friction Band

- **Pacing Mode:** `Crisis (Crisis Time)`. High-tempo, simultaneous round-by-round combat and environmental disruption where every action must balance offensive pressure, defensive survival, and objective progress against the ritual’s impending completion.
- **Friction Band:** `Crisis / Lethal Crucible`
- **Cost & Stamina Expectation:** Second-by-second life-or-death melee against cult champions and a 30-foot awakening colossus under a hard countdown. Massive physical exertion and high risk of Severity 3+ crushing trauma or internal hemorrhage.
- **Abort & Failure Dynamic:** Aborting means fleeing the sanctum, yielding the pass, and permitting the colossus to awaken and devastate the region.

### Plausible Narrative Fallout Spectrum

- **Clean Outcome:** Masterful execution. The conduits are severed in the opening moments; the colossus slumps back onto its dais inert; the vanguard breaks the champions' line; the hierophant is captured; party suffers only minor cuts and bruises.
- **Minor Friction:** Costly victory. The colossus partially awakens, lashing out with a massive stone arm that shatters a pillar before the ritual is severed; falling masonry strikes the vanguard (`Concussive Impact / Cracked Shield`); the scholar overextends their magical reserves (`Deep Arcane Burnout`); the cultists are slain, but the sanctum ceiling is unstable and raining dust.
- **Severe Consequence:** Catastrophic near-failure. The colossus awakens enough to take several steps, stomping through the floor; the vanguard must throw themselves into a desperate last stand to hold the creature's foot while the scout severs the core crystal; the colossus collapses in a massive avalanche of stone that buries the vanguard, inflicting crushing trauma (`Crushed Leg / Internal Hemorrhage`); the temple is collapsing into the chasm.

### Engine Brainstorming Prompts: Coupling & Cross-Domain Bleed

1. **Multi-Track Objectives in Crisis:**
   - In a crisis that mixes martial combat with objective disruption (`Disrupt a Ritual`), how does the engine manage player attention? If one player is solely fighting for their life on the bridge while another is targeting ritual nodes, how do their cards or resolution mechanisms interact?
2. **The Immediate Weight of Severe Trauma:**
   - When the vanguard suffers a crushed leg or cracked ribs during this fight, how does the engine ensure the consequence is immediately felt in play without completely sidelining the player from the game?

## Beat 4: Dusk Post-Crisis Extraction: The Burdened Retreat

### Narrative Context & Stakes

The Vault of Echoes is collapsing behind them; tremors shake the cliffside as dust plows out of the shattered antechamber. Outside, the alpine sun is sinking below the jagged horizon, plunging the peaks into sub-zero twilight. A blizzard is howling across the upper pass.

The party’s victory has come at a brutal physical price. The vanguard’s leg is severely fractured, and they cannot walk unaided; their armor is dented and too heavy to bear. Surving cultist trackers and mountain scavengers are following the sound of the collapse. To survive the night, the scout and scholar must splint the broken limb, build an emergency travois sled from broken lances and ropes, haul 250 pounds of wounded warrior down crumbling scree slopes, lay false tracks to throw off pursuit, and locate an airtight mountain cave to establish a survivable hearth.

### Actions & Colors Tagged

- `Administer Aid` ([[Yellow]] - Combat / Exploration): Setting a compound bone fracture, applying pressure bandages to staunch arterial bleeding, and stabilizing the patient for transport.
- `Carry a Burden` ([[Red]] - Exploration): Hauling the heavy travois sled carrying the incapacitated comrade across miles of boulder fields and freezing scree.
- `Create False Trail` ([[Yellow]] - Exploration): Laying broken tracks, scuff marks, and false rope anchors toward a dead-end ravine to mislead tracking mountain scouts.
- `Set up Camp` ([[Yellow]] - Exploration): Finding a dry, defensible cave fissure, sealing the entrance against blizzard drafts, and kindling a hot, smokeless fire from alpine scrub and fatwood.

### Pacing Mode & Friction Band

- **Pacing Mode:** `General Action (Adventuring Time) / Post-Crisis Extraction`. The immediate combat rounds have ended, but survival is more precarious than ever. Actions take hours, every mile requires physical grit, and mistakes carry lethal environmental consequences.
- **Friction Band:** `Taxing / Moderate Friction` (escalating to `Perilous / High Friction` under pursuit and blizzard exposure)
- **Cost & Stamina Expectation:** Grinding physical endurance ordeal hauling an immobilized comrade down scree in a blizzard. Steady stamina burn; incurs multiple Severity 1–2 physical exhaustion conditions (`Severe Muscle Strain / Exhausted Shoulders`).
- **Abort & Failure Dynamic:** Refusing to pace the travois or lighten loads risks hypothermic collapse. Players must make hard triage trade-offs (abandoning heavy packs or captured relics, seeking cold drafty shelter) to avoid lethal exposure.

### Plausible Narrative Fallout Spectrum

- **Clean Outcome:** Heroic extraction. The splint holds; the travois glides smoothly over snowbanks; the pursuers are thrown completely off track by the false trail; a secure, dry cavern is found where a warm hearth is established, stabilizing the wounded warrior through the night.
- **Minor Friction:** Exhausting endurance ordeal. Hauling the heavy sled through snowdrifts exhausts the scout and scholar, leaving them running on pure adrenaline (`Severe Muscle Strain / Exhausted Shoulders`); the storm intensifies, drenching firewood and requiring desperate effort to ignite; minor re-injury of the fracture from a sled jolt.
- **Severe Consequence:** Desperate struggle against exposure and pursuit. The sled breaks on sharp scree; pursuers catch the scent and launch a harassing arrow volley; the party is forced to abandon heavy packs and captured relics to haul the vanguard on their shoulders through waist-deep snow; they reach a freezing, drafty shelter barely in time, with the vanguard slipping into hypothermic shock.

### Engine Brainstorming Prompts: Coupling & Cross-Domain Bleed

1. **Trauma Inverting Routine General Actions:**
   - On a clear morning, walking down an alpine path or pitching a tent is a trivial routine task requiring little thought. But with a bleeding companion on a makeshift sled, a broken limb, and a blizzard howling, **routine movement becomes the most dramatic and dangerous challenge of the expedition**.
   - How does the engine ensure that post-crisis wounds transform ordinary exploration into tense, high-stakes trade-offs without needing a whole separate sub-system?
2. **Burden Drag & Shared Exhaustion:**
   - Carrying a wounded ally is not a personal state; it is a shared physical burden (`Carry a Burden`). How does the engine distribute this mechanical drag across the uninjured party members?
   - Does hauling the sled drain the scout's agility or the scholar's concentration? If attacked during extraction, how does protecting an immobilized patient warp the defensive options available?

## Summary Evaluation Rubric for Engine Designers

When validating a candidate resolution engine against this scenario, ensure it answers these core design questions cleanly:

| Invariant                     | Design Challenge                                                                                                            | Narrative Test                                                                                                          |
| :---------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Environmental Harm Bleed**  | Does physical trauma suffered during travel (twisted ankle, hypothermia) realistically degrade combat defense and mobility? | Does a vanguard who cracked a rib crossing an icy beam struggle to parry a heavy blow in the sanctum?                   |
| **Reconnaissance Trade-Offs** | Does spending universal stamina to case an objective grant tangible, tactical advantages when the Crisis erupts?            | Does finding an acoustic flaw or structural fracture during exploration provide direct leverage to topple the colossus? |
