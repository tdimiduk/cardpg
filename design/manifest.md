# Preamble: Master Document Index

This document is the master index of all design documents for the project. Its use is governed by the `Knowledge Manifest Protocol` as defined in the Chronicler & Artificer's core prompt. Each document is assigned a `Status` (`Canon`, `Leading-Edge`, or `Archived`) to guide its interpretation and use.

# Core Documents (Load by Default)

- **[Guiding Principles](philosophy/guiding-principles.md):** The "Why." The ultimate authority on the project's creative vision. (Type: Design Document) (Status: Canon)
- **[Core Rules](rules/core-rules.md):** The "How." The foundational mechanics of the game. (Type: Game Rules) (Status: Canon)
- **[Design Precepts](philosophy/design-precepts.md):** The "How-To." Patterns and case studies for designing new mechanics. (Type: Design Document) (Status: Leading-Edge)

# Situational Documents (Request as Needed)

- **[Sources of Inspiration](research/inspiration-sources.md):** The "From Where." A list of media and their key design lessons. (Type: Inspiration Library) (Status: Canon)
- **[Verisimilitude Sources](research/verisimilitude-sources.md):** The "Factual Basis." Historical and anthropological sources for world-building. (Type: Factual Bedrock) (Status: Canon)
- **[Gamemaster's Guide](rules/gamemaster-guide.md):** Instructions for how to run the game, particularly relevant when writing new cards or thinking about how the game flows. (Type: GM Procedures) (Status: Canon)
- **[Colors of Action](rules/colors-of-action.md):** A player-facing lexicon used to classify any in-game action as Red, Yellow, or Blue. (Type: Action Lexicon) (Status: Canon)
- **[Clocks and Fronts](rules/modules/clocks-and-fronts.md):** Rules module with information on the Clocks and Fronts campaign play system. (Type: Game Rules Module) (Status: Canon)
- **[Snap Check](rules/modules/snap-check.md):** Rules module with information on the Snap Check system to handle things like "passive" perception. (Type: Game Rules Module) (Status: Canon)
- **[Armor Research](research/armor-research.md):** Summary of historical research on armor, only relevant to discussing rules for armor or consequences to characters in armor. (Type: Research Summary) (Status: Canon)
- **[Design Sketchbook](iteration/design-sketchbook.md):** A collection of specific mechanic ideas, interesting concepts, and design explorations. (Type: Ideation) (Status: Canon)
