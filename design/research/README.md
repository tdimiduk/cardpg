# Research Directory

This directory is the factual bedrock of our "Casual Realism" guiding principle. It contains the evidence-based analyses and source materials we use to ensure our game mechanics feel grounded and intuitive. Its purpose is to house objective, analytical work that serves as a foundation for design, separating the "what is real" from the "how we make it fun."

## Key Documents & Subdirectories

A new designer or contributor should start here to understand our factual basis:

- **[verisimilitude-sources.yaml](verisimilitude-sources.yaml)**: The master list of our vetted historical and scientific sources. This is the starting point for any new research task.
- **[reports](reports)**: This subdirectory contains the full, deep-dive research reports. These are the primary sources for our design work.
- **[synthesis](synthesis)**: This subdirectory contains concise, actionable design summaries. Documents here translate the raw data from the `/reports` directory into a practical framework for designing rules and content.

## Contribution Guidelines

To maintain the integrity and focus of this directory, all contributions must adhere to the following principles:

- **Purpose:** The goal of any document in this directory is to provide factual, evidence-based information.
- **Style:** Submissions should be objective and analytical. Prioritize clear data presentation using tables, timelines, and quantitative analysis where possible.
- **Quantification Standard:** When researching physical exertion, prioritize quantifiable metrics of energy expenditure. **Metabolic Equivalent of Task (METs)** has proven to be a highly effective, system-agnostic unit for comparing the costs of diverse actions and should be actively sought in source material.
- **Constraint:** **Do not propose specific game mechanics here.** This directory is for the "factual basis" only. All mechanic design, brainstorming, and iteration belongs in the `/iteration` directory.
