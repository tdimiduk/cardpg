# Arcane & Alchemical Consequence Database

This file serves as a structured, rules-independent database of magical backdrafts, alchemical toxicity, elemental exposure, and runic failures for **caRdPG**, grounded in thermodynamic principles, chemical toxicity, and cognitive psychology.

Refer to the central [Consequence Database Hub](file:///home/tdimiduk/cardpg/cardpg/design/research/synthesis/consequence-database.md) for general guidelines.

## Domain-Specific Calibration Brackets

### Onset Impact (1-100) - Arcane and Alchemical:

- **1–10 (Minor)**: Minor magical residue, static charge, slight discoloration, or minor chemical smell. Annoying but does not impair casting or physical action (e.g., Singed Sleeves, Static Spark).
- **11–30 (Moderate)**: Light elemental burns, temporary sensory shifts (seeing colors, ringing ears from thunderclap), or moderate alchemical nausea. Slightly slows spellcasting or dexterity (e.g., Alchemical Nausea, Frost Nip, Mana Static).
- **31–60 (Severe)**: Severe magical backdraft. Severe elemental burns, temporary blindness/deafness, spellcasting block, or localized tissue crystallization (e.g., Pyric Backdraft, Runic Backlash, Cryogenic Frost Nip).
- **61–90 (Critical)**: Life-threatening magical anomaly or severe poisoning. Systemic mana burnout, organ crystallization, cognitive instability, or severe alchemical acid damage (e.g., Mana Burnout, Alchemical Acid Blast, Planar Distortion Shock).
- **91–100 (Fatal)**: Instant obliteration or permanent total mind-shatter (e.g., Arcane Disintegration, Permanent Mind Void).

### Recovery Difficulty (1-100) - Arcane and Alchemical:

- **1–10 (Trivial)**: Resolves naturally in minutes/hours with basic grounding, deep breaths, or washing skin.
- **11–30 (Minor)**: Resolves in hours to 1 week with basic herbal teas, standard ointments, or rest.
- **31–60 (Moderate)**: Resolves in weeks; requires specialized alchemical neutralizers, magic-nullifying environments, or clinical care.
- **61–90 (Major)**: Resolves in months; requires rare magical/alchemical antidotes, deep planar therapy, or specialized surgical intervention.
- **91–100 (Permanent)**: Permanent magic loss, irreversible tissue corruption, or complete loss of mind.
