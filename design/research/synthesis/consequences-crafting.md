## Domain-Specific Calibration Brackets

### Onset Impact (1-100) - Crafting and Labor:

- **1–10 (Minor)**: Minor wear-and-tear, tool dulling, slight muscle fatigue/finger soreness. Doesn't stop work but is annoying (e.g., Sore Fingers, Nicked Blade).
- **11–30 (Moderate)**: Noticeable tool/material damage, light burns, or hand strain that slows production or slightly degrades work quality (e.g., Solder Singe, Warped Wood, Repetitive Wrist Strain).
- **31–60 (Severe)**: Major tool breakage, ruined rare raw materials, severe hand burns, chemical inhalation, or extreme fatigue that halts work (e.g., Cracked Anvil, Ruined Silk Batch, Scalded Palm, Inhaled Fumes).
- **61–90 (Critical)**: Catastrophic workplace accident (forge explosion, structural collapse, toxin exposure), severe chemical burn, or systemic physical collapse from sleep deprivation (e.g., Acid-Splashed Hands, Autonomic Exhaustion).
- **91–100 (Fatal)**: Fatal workplace incident or permanent occupational disability (e.g., losing fingers to a gear, permanent blindness from sparks).

### Recovery Difficulty (1-100) - Crafting and Labor:

- **1–10 (Trivial)**: Resolves with simple tool sharpening or a short break (minutes/hours).
- **11–30 (Minor)**: Resolves in hours to a week with basic tool repairs, replacing minor parts, or 1-2 nights of solid rest.
- **31–60 (Moderate)**: Resolves in weeks; requires smelting/re-forging tools, sourcing rare replacement components, or clinical wound care.
- **61–90 (Major)**: Resolves in months; requires reconstructing an entire workshop/forge, major medical/surgical recovery, or expensive alchemical neutralizers.
- **91–100 (Permanent)**: Permanent loss of workshop, destruction of a legendary tool, or permanent loss of a crafting skill (due to amputation/blindness).
