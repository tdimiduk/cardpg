# Logistics & Equipment Consequence Database

This file serves as a structured, rules-independent database of equipment wear, transport breakdowns, supply contamination, and pack animal injuries for **caRdPG**, grounded in historical transportation, animal husbandry, metallurgy, and material science.

Refer to the central [Consequence Database Hub](file:///home/tdimiduk/cardpg/cardpg/design/research/synthesis/consequence-database.md) for general guidelines.

## Domain-Specific Calibration Brackets

### Onset Impact (1-100) - Logistics and Equipment:

- **1–10 (Minor)**: Minor wear and tear, loose buckles, or slightly damp supplies. Annoying but easily managed (e.g., Loose Strap, Damp Rations).
- **11–30 (Moderate)**: Noticeable equipment damage or pack animal strain. Slows travel speed or slightly limits carrying capacity (e.g., Rusty Buckles, Frayed Lead Rope, Hoof Abscess).
- **31–60 (Severe)**: Major tool/carriage breakdown or animal injury. Halts travel or renders a primary item completely unusable until repaired (e.g., Broken Wagon Axle, Worn-Out Boots, Spoiled Feed Batch).
- **61–90 (Critical)**: Catastrophic logistical failure. Loss of major carriage/cargo, death of primary pack animal, or complete destruction of survival inventory (e.g., Wagon Rollover, Poisoned Feed/Water Source).
- **91–100 (Fatal)**: Absolute total loss of all transport and supplies, leaving the party completely stranded and exposed without tools (e.g., Complete Supply Washout).

### Recovery Difficulty (1-100) - Logistics and Equipment:

- **1–10 (Trivial)**: Resolves in minutes with basic tightening, quick drying, or simple tool adjustments.
- **11–30 (Minor)**: Resolves in hours to a week with basic repair kits, replacing small leather straps, or resting animals.
- **31–60 (Moderate)**: Resolves in weeks; requires wood/metalworking skills, blacksmithing tools, or veterinary treatment for animals.
- **61–90 (Major)**: Resolves in months; requires rebuilding carts from scratch, purchasing new pack beasts, or importing specialized materials.
- **91–100 (Permanent)**: Permanent loss of legendary gear, complete annihilation of vehicle, or death of a prized mount.
