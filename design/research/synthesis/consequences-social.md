# Social Consequence Database

This file serves as a structured, rules-independent database of social fallout, reputational damage, and psychological/cognitive stress states for **caRdPG**, grounded in historical sociology, interpersonal relations, and psychological dynamics.

Refer to the central [Consequence Database Hub](file:///home/tdimiduk/cardpg/cardpg/design/research/synthesis/consequence-database.md) for general guidelines.

---

## Domain-Specific Calibration Brackets

### Onset Impact (1-100) - Social and Psychological:

- **1–10 (Minor)**: Minor social awkwardness, fleeting embarrassment, mild nervous tension, or minor etiquette faux pas easily shrugged off (e.g., Fumbled Greeting, Slip of the Tongue).
- **11–30 (Moderate)**: Visible loss of face, a snub by peers, moderate anxiety/panic, or light reputational damage that impairs social interactions or increases stress (e.g., Cold Shoulder, Public Snub, Mild Paranoia).
- **31–60 (Severe)**: Significant social isolation, major loss of credibility, heavy blackmail pressure, or severe panic/anxiety attacks that prevent logical thought/action (e.g., Exposed Lie, Blackmail Threat, Acute Panic Attack).
- **61–90 (Critical)**: Near-total social ruin, target of active witch-hunt/inquisition, or severe psychological trauma resulting in catatonia or loss of agency (e.g., Accused of Treason, Exile Verdict, Hysterical Blindness).
- **91–100 (Fatal)**: Complete and irreversible social death (shunned permanently by all society, execution order) or permanent mind shatter (e.g., Outlawry/Declaration of Wolf's Head, Permanent Insanity).

### Recovery Difficulty (1-100) - Social and Psychological:

- **1–10 (Trivial)**: Resolved with a quick apology, deep breaths, or immediate distance/avoidance (minutes).
- **11–30 (Minor)**: Resolved in days with minor face-saving deeds, private apologies, or restful sleep (hours to 1 week).
- **31–60 (Moderate)**: Resolved in weeks to months; requires significant favors, public service, or professional psychological counseling/isolation (1 week to 2 months).
- **61–90 (Major)**: Resolved in months to a year; requires major heroic/political intervention, clearing one's name at high court, or profound trauma counseling (2 months to 1 year).
- **91–100 (Permanent)**: Permanent, irreversible reputation loss (infamy/heresy) or permanent psychological damage.
