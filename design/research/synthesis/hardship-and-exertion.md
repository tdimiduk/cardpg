# Hardship and Exertion

This synthesis establishes the empirical and physiological baseline for functional degradation caused by load carriage, acute joint trauma, overuse kinetics, metabolic exhaustion, and environmental deprivation in austere settings. It documents the physical timescales, metabolic thresholds, tissue recovery curves, and observable capability losses that govern human performance under duress.

---

## Core Findings from Empirical Research

1. **Proprioceptive Deficit as Sensorimotor Instability:**
   Ligament tears (e.g., ankle or knee sprains) destroy embedded mechanoreceptors (Ruffini endings, Pacinian corpuscles, Golgi-like organs) that feed joint position sense and tension into the central nervous system ([`mattacola-2002`](file:///home/tdimiduk/cardpg/cardpg/design/research/sources/verisimilitude/mattacola-2002-rehabilitation-ankle-sprain-instability/extracted.md); [`wang-2022`](file:///home/tdimiduk/cardpg/cardpg/design/research/sources/verisimilitude/wang-2022-knee-ligament-proprioceptive-recovery/extracted.md)). This loss of afferent sensory signaling doubles reflexive stabilization latency (e.g., hamstring reaction time doubling from $40\ \text{ms}$ to $>80\ \text{ms}$) and induces chronic functional instability in up to 73% of cases. The functional consequence of a sprain is not merely localized pain, but a lost capacity for autonomous joint stabilization, manifesting as increased postural sway, altered gait, and frequent unprovoked "giving way" on uneven terrain.

2. **Central Cognitive Attrition Under Load Carriage:**
   Loaded locomotion is a dual-task stressor where physical propulsion and cognitive executive processing compete for shared neural resources ([`drain-2023`](file:///home/tdimiduk/cardpg/cardpg/design/research/sources/verisimilitude/drain-2023-cognitive-performance-load-carriage/extracted.md)). In military trials under 40–61 kg loads, working memory accuracy on $n$-back tests fell from 98% down to 62% over 3 hours, and vigilance/inhibition on Go/No Go tasks degraded substantially. Prolonged load carriage erodes prefrontal executive function (working memory, route tracking, environmental hazard detection) independently of peripheral muscular capacity.

3. **Glycogen Depletion vs. Starvation Ketosis ("The Bonk"):**
   Heavy load carriage burns $500\text{–}800\ \text{kcal/hr}$ ([`askew-2012`](file:///home/tdimiduk/cardpg/cardpg/design/research/verisimilitude-sources.yaml); [`murray-2018`](file:///home/tdimiduk/cardpg/cardpg/design/research/sources/verisimilitude/murray-2018-fundamentals-glycogen-metabolism/extracted.md)). At intensities $>65\%\ \text{VO}_{2\text{max}}$, skeletal muscle and liver glycogen reserves are exhausted within **2 to 3 hours** without continuous carbohydrate intake. Fasting ketosis requires 24–48 hours of starvation to establish; during a grueling single-day trek, unsupplied travelers suffer acute exercise-induced hypoglycemia ("bonking"), characterized by heavy limbs ("lead legs"), loss of motor power, and neuroglycopenic brain fog. Short rest pauses (10–15 minutes) restore acute anaerobic phosphagens and clear lactic acidosis, but cannot restore muscle glycogen without dietary carbohydrate consumption and prolonged rest.

4. **The Alimentary Ceiling on Sustainable Daily Exertion:**
   Human endurance over multi-day operations is bounded by a metabolic ceiling of **$\approx 2.5\times \text{Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)}$** ($\approx 4,500\ \text{kcal/day}$ for a 75 kg individual; $\approx 25\text{–}30$ recoverable MET-hours), governed by the maximum absorptive capacity of the human gastrointestinal tract ([`thurber-2019`](file:///home/tdimiduk/cardpg/cardpg/design/research/sources/verisimilitude/thurber-2019-alimentary-limit-human-endurance/extracted.md)). Daily workloads exceeding this ceiling create an unrecoverable catabolic debt that forces tissue breakdown, cumulative chronic fatigue, and rapid immune collapse.

5. **Overuse Kinetics Follow Deterministic Stages:**
   Pathologies like friction blisters, stress fractures, and trench foot accumulate through cyclic microtrauma and hygiene neglect ([`rushton-2024`](file:///home/tdimiduk/cardpg/cardpg/design/research/sources/verisimilitude/rushton-2024-friction-blister-causation/extracted.md); [`sharma-2019`](file:///home/tdimiduk/cardpg/cardpg/design/research/sources/verisimilitude/sharma-2019-stress-fractures-military-recruits/extracted.md); [`zafren-2021`](file:///home/tdimiduk/cardpg/cardpg/design/research/sources/verisimilitude/zafren-2021-nonfreezing-cold-injury-trench-foot/extracted.md)). Repetitive shear strain progresses predictably to intraepidermal fluid clefts; cyclic bone microcracks outpace osteoblast remodeling to form cortical stress fractures; and continuous damp-cold immersion transitions from initial protective numbness to severe microvascular and neuropathic ischemia.

### Central Fatigue Modulation Model

Central mental fatigue acts as a multiplier on physical perceived exertion:
$$\text{RPE}_{\text{perceived}} = \text{RPE}_{\text{physical}} \times (1 + k \cdot \text{MentalFatigue})$$
Under moderate-to-severe cognitive fatigue ($k \cdot \text{MentalFatigue} \approx 0.2\text{–}0.4$), time to exhaustion (TTE) in sustained endurance tasks drops by **$15\%\text{ to }25\%$**, even while cardiovascular capacity ($\text{VO}_{2\text{max}}$) remains physically intact ([`tornero-aguilera-2022`](file:///home/tdimiduk/cardpg/cardpg/design/research/sources/verisimilitude/tornero-aguilera-2022-central-peripheral-fatigue-review/extracted.md)).

### Quantitative Fuel Reserves & Kinetics

- **Liver Glycogen:** $\approx 80\text{–}100\ \text{grams}$ ($\approx 320\text{–}400\ \text{kcal}$). Acts as the systemic blood glucose buffer to sustain central nervous system and brain function ([`murray-2018`](file:///home/tdimiduk/cardpg/cardpg/design/research/sources/verisimilitude/murray-2018-fundamentals-glycogen-metabolism/extracted.md)).
- **Skeletal Muscle Glycogen:** $\approx 350\text{–}500\ \text{grams}$ ($\approx 1,400\text{–}2,000\ \text{kcal}$). Stored within individual myocytes and locked into local contraction; cannot be transferred to blood glucose.
- **Armored & Loaded Burn Rates:** Marching under armor or pack loads consumes $500\text{–}800\ \text{kcal/hr}$ ([`askew-2012`](file:///home/tdimiduk/cardpg/cardpg/design/research/verisimilitude-sources.yaml)). Carbohydrate oxidation consumes glycogen at $1.5\text{–}3.0\ \text{g/min}$ at $>65\%\ \text{VO}_{2\text{max}}$.
- **Depletion Window:** Locomotor muscle glycogen drops to near zero within **2 to 3 hours** without continuous carbohydrate intake.
- **Starvation Ketosis:** Ketone adaptation requires **24 to 48 hours** of fasting. On single-day operations, individuals do not enter ketosis; they suffer acute exercise-induced hypoglycemia.
- **The Alimentary Ceiling:** Total sustainable daily energy expenditure over multi-day operations caps at **$\approx 2.5\times \text{BMR}$** ($\approx 4,500\ \text{kcal/day}$), bounded by gut absorption limits ([`thurber-2019`](file:///home/tdimiduk/cardpg/cardpg/design/research/sources/verisimilitude/thurber-2019-alimentary-limit-human-endurance/extracted.md)). Exertion beyond 25–30 MET-hours/day incurs catabolic tissue debt.
- **Rest Intervals vs. Fuel Resynthesis:**
  - **Phosphocreatine (PCr):** 95% resynthesized within **3 to 5 minutes** of rest.
  - **Lactate & Metabolic Acidosis:** Cleared in **30 to 60 minutes** during active recovery ($t_{1/2} \approx 7\text{–}8\ \text{min}$).
  - **Glycogen Resynthesis:** Proceeds at only **5–10 mmol/kg/hr** under optimal carbohydrate feeding; full restoration requires **24 to 48 hours** with adequate nutrition. Brief rest pauses relieve acute metabolic acidosis and replenish sprint phosphagens, but cannot reverse systemic glycogen depletion without carbohydrate feeding and extended downtime.
