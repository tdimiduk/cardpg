# Keyword Glossary

This document is a quick reference for the core mechanical terms used in the game. These keywords have specific, consistent meanings within the rules.

## `Color`

How approaches and abilities are classified and actions are adjudicated. Values are `Red`, `Yellow`, and `Blue`. See [Colors of Action](colors-of-action.md) for lots of examples of how these are classified.

### `Red`

One of the game's `Colors`. The color of Force, Endurance, Presence, Passion, Dominion. 


### `Yellow`

One of the game's `Colors`. The color of Speed, Precision, Perception, Cunning, Finesse

### `Blue`

One of the game's `Colors`. The color of Intellect, Planning, Discipline, Lore, Intrigue

## `Defense`

One of the key stats for resolving actions. Defense tells you how many cards of `Impact` translate into taking one consequence.

## `Impact`

The total number of cards flipped from the deck during a defend or general action. `Impact` is divided by `Defense` to determine the number of consequences suffered."

## `Resilience`

The number of consequences a character can suffer at a given severity level before new consequences increase to the next level.

## `Strength`

How strong/powerful and action is. Strength will be set by a character's action, a challenge card, or a gamemaster adjudication. The character carrying out a general action or defending against an attack must meet the strength of the attack in their action resolution.
