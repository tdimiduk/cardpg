# Keyword Glossary

This document is a quick reference for core mechanical terms used in the game. These keywords have specific, consistent meanings within the rules.

## The Three Colors

### Color

_Aliases: Colors_

How approaches and abilities are classified and actions are adjudicated. Values are `Red`, `Yellow`, and `Blue`. See [Colors of Action](colors-of-action.md) for detailed classifications.

#### Red

One of the game's [[Colors]]. The color of Force, Endurance, Presence, Passion, Dominion.

#### Yellow

One of the game's [[Colors]]. The color of Speed, Precision, Perception, Cunning, Finesse.

#### Blue

One of the game's [[Colors]]. The color of Intellect, Planning, Discipline, Lore, Intrigue.

## Action Resolution

### Strength

How strong or powerful an action or hazard is. Set by a character's action card, a challenge card, or GM adjudication. The character carrying out a general action or defending against an attack must meet the strength of the challenge in their action resolution.

### Impact

The total number of cards flipped from the deck during a defense or general action. [[Impact]] is divided by [[Defense]] to determine the number of consequences suffered.

### Defense

One of the key stats for resolving actions. [[Defense]] tells you how many cards of [[Impact]] translate into taking one [[Consequence]]. Unarmored characters default to [[Defense]] 1.

### Resilience

The number of [[Consequences]] a character can suffer at a given [[Severity]] level before new consequences escalate to the next severity tier. Defaults to 1 if unarmored.

### Consequence

_Aliases: Consequences_

Cards suffered from physical trauma, strain, or environmental friction when [[Impact]] meets or exceeds [[Defense]]. Suffix/plural uses [[Consequences]].

### Severity

_Aliases: Severity 1, Severity 2, Severity 3_

The escalation tier of suffered [[Consequences]]. Severity 1 represents minor friction/wear; Severity 2 represents serious injury/vulnerability; Severity 3 indicates incapacitation.

## Equipment & Fatigue

### Burden

The metabolic cost of armor and heavy equipment. When you perform a [[Fatigue Cycle]], you must add extra [[Fatigue]] cards to your deck equal to your total [[Burden]].

### Fatigue

_Aliases: Fatigue Cycle, Fatigue Cards_

Systemic physical exhaustion. When your deck is exhausted, a [[Fatigue Cycle]] adds [[Fatigue]] cards (plus active [[Burden]]) to your expended pile before reshuffling.

## Pacing & Play Modes

### Crisis Time

Turn-based, round-by-round mode for resolving combat, chases, and tense second-by-second struggles.

### Adventuring Time

Fluid exploration mode used for travel, dungeon navigation, investigation, and downtime.

### Downtime

Extended periods of rest, crafting, project advancement, and long-term recovery between active adventures.

### Ready Hand

A pool of 0 to 4 cards maintained on the table during [[Adventuring Time]] representing immediate vigilance and readiness.

### Action Stack

_Aliases: Action Stacks_

An Action card played from hand combined with additional cards committed to pay its resource cost.

### General Action

_Aliases: General Actions_

Non-combat challenges with meaningful stakes outside combat, resolved defender-centric with success as default.

## Action Types & Card Keywords

### Attack

_Aliases: Attacks_

An offensive action declared in [[Crisis Time]] targeting another character, setting a [[Strength]] in a chosen [[Color]].

### Defend

_Aliases: Defends_

A defensive reaction played or resolved to absorb incoming [[Strength]].

### Stance

_Aliases: Stances_

A persistent tactical posture remaining active across rounds until discarded or replaced.

### Passive

A card property that is continuously active while the card is in play or held in hand.
